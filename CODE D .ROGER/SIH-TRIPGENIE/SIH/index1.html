🌍 About Trip Genie

Project Summary

Trip Genie is a smart travel assistance platform designed to make travelling easier, faster and more convenient. It helps tourists discover destinations, plan journeys, access useful travel information and connect with travel service providers through one simple platform.

Our Goal

Our goal is to bring important travel assistance into one convenient place and reduce the time and effort needed for trip planning.

Advantages

  • Simple and user-friendly interface.
  • Helps tourists discover destinations and attractions.
  • Reduces travel-planning time.
  • Provides useful information in one place.
  • Connects tourists with travel service providers.
  • Can support multilingual communication.
